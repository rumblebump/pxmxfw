WireGuard tunnels

		

			Each tunnel is an interface with a role, like a NIC. With role lan, the networks behind its peers and your other lans reach each other,
			which is how you join Proxmox nodes or sites. Peers' allowed networks are routed into the tunnel (never a default route).

WireGuard tunnels

		

			Each tunnel is an interface like a NIC: rules under Firewall say what passes through it.
			Forwarding rules between a tunnel and your LANs join Proxmox nodes or sites. Peers' allowed networks are routed into the tunnel (never a default route).
